# Campus Handbook

CreamCollar Institute of Technology (CCIT) — General information for students.
This handbook covers day-to-day campus matters. It does not define how examination results or attendance are analysed.

## CH-1 Library

The central library is open from 8 am to 10 pm on working days and from 9 am to 5 pm on Saturdays. An undergraduate student may borrow up to four books for fourteen days. A fine of two rupees per day is charged for each overdue book, and borrowing is suspended once the unpaid fine reaches fifty rupees.

## CH-2 Hostel

Hostel residents must return by 9:30 pm. Visitors are allowed in the common room between 4 pm and 7 pm. A resident who is away for more than two nights must inform the warden in writing. Electrical appliances above 750 watts are not permitted in rooms.

## CH-3 Fees and refunds

Tuition fees are paid in two instalments, in July and in January. A late fee of forty rupees per day is charged after the due date. A student who withdraws within fifteen days of admission is refunded 75 percent of the tuition fee; no refund is made after that.

## CH-4 Examination hall conduct

Students must be seated fifteen minutes before an examination begins and are not admitted after the first thirty minutes. Mobile phones, smart watches and programmable calculators are not allowed in the hall. A student found with unauthorised material is reported to the malpractice committee.

## CH-5 Anti-ragging

Ragging in any form is prohibited on and off campus. Complaints can be made to the anti-ragging committee in person, by email or through the anonymous box outside the academic office, and are acted upon within twenty-four hours.

## CH-6 Sports and cultural activities

The institute fields teams in cricket, football, basketball, badminton and athletics. Students selected for a university team are excused from classes on match days under the duty leave rule. The annual cultural festival is held in February and the technical festival in September.

## CH-7 Financial scholarships

The institute awards need-based scholarships that cover up to 50 percent of the tuition fee. To retain a scholarship in the following year, a student must have an aggregate of at least 60 percent and no pending disciplinary case. Scholarship applications close on 31 August each year.

## CH-8 Grievance redressal

A student may raise an academic or administrative grievance with the class mentor. If it is not resolved within seven working days it is escalated to the Head of Department, and after a further seven working days to the grievance redressal cell, whose decision is final.

## CH-9 Acceptable use of computing facilities

Computing laboratories and the campus network are provided for academic work. Students must not share their login credentials, attempt to access another person's files, or install unlicensed software. Network activity is logged, and misuse can lead to suspension of access.

## CH-10 Transport

The institute runs buses on twelve city routes. Bus passes are issued per semester and are not transferable. The first bus leaves the campus at 4:45 pm and the last at 6:15 pm on working days.

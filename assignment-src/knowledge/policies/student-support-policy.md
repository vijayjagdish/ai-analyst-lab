# Student Support Policy

CreamCollar Institute of Technology (CCIT) — Academic Support and Mentoring, Batch 2024.
This policy defines which students the institute must support, and where they are referred.

## SS-1 Academic attention in a subject

A student needs academic attention in a subject when at least one of the following is true for that subject:

- the student has not passed the paper, which includes being absent for it (see the pass marks and absent rules of the Academic Regulations), or
- the student's recorded attendance in the subject is below 75 percent.

A record whose attendance is not recorded is judged on the marks condition alone. Academic attention is decided per subject: one student can need attention in several subjects.

## SS-2 At-risk students

A student is at risk in a semester when the student needs academic attention (SS-1) in two or more subjects of that same semester.

At-risk status is decided semester by semester. A student with one flagged subject in semester 3 and one in semester 4 is not at risk in either semester.

## SS-3 Priority counselling

An at-risk student (SS-2) who also has a severe attendance shortage, meaning recorded attendance below 65 percent, in at least one subject of that semester is placed on the priority counselling list. The class mentor must meet the student within one week of the list being published.

## SS-4 Referral for academic attention

A student who needs academic attention in a subject is referred as follows:

- if a remedial (bridge) course supports that subject, the student is referred to that course and to its coordinator;
- otherwise the student is referred to the faculty member who teaches the subject.

The remedial courses, the subjects each one supports, their coordinators and the teaching allocation are maintained in the curriculum knowledge graph. They are not listed in any policy document, and they must be looked up there.

## SS-5 Prerequisite risk

A student who has a backlog in a subject is at prerequisite risk in every later subject that depends on it, whether directly or through a chain of prerequisites. For example, a backlog in a semester 3 subject puts the student at prerequisite risk in the semester 5 subjects that build on it.

Mentors must advise such students before course registration. The prerequisite structure of the curriculum is maintained in the curriculum knowledge graph and must be looked up there.

## SS-6 Dean's merit list

A student is placed on the Dean's merit list for a semester when all of the following hold for that semester:

- the semester aggregate (Academic Regulations, semester aggregate of a student) is at least 80 percent;
- the student has passed every paper of the semester;
- the recorded attendance is at least 85 percent in every paper of the semester. Papers whose attendance is not recorded are ignored for this condition.

## SS-7 Mentoring meetings and confidentiality

Every student is assigned a faculty mentor at admission. Mentors meet their students at least twice a semester and record the discussion in the mentoring register. Lists that name individual students who need academic attention are shared only with the mentor, the Head of Department and the academic office; summaries prepared for wider circulation must use counts rather than names.

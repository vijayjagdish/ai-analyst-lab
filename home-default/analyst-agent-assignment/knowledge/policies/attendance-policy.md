# Attendance Policy

CreamCollar Institute of Technology (CCIT) — Attendance Regulations, Batch 2024.
Attendance is recorded per student, per subject, as a percentage of the classes conducted in that subject during the semester.

## AT-1 Minimum attendance requirement

A student must attend at least 75 percent of the classes conducted in each subject. The requirement applies to every subject separately; a high attendance in one subject does not compensate for a low attendance in another.

A recorded attendance below 75 in a subject is an attendance shortage in that subject.

## AT-2 Shortage categories

An attendance shortage is classified by how far below the requirement it is:

- Condonable shortage: attendance of at least 65 but below 75. The Head of Department may condone it once per semester on documented medical grounds.
- Severe shortage: attendance below 65. It cannot be condoned and the student must repeat the course work of that subject during the summer term.

Both categories are attendance shortages under AT-1.

## AT-3 Attendance that is not recorded

When the `attendance` value of a record is empty, the attendance register for that student and subject has not yet been verified by the department office.

An empty attendance value is not a shortage. Such records are left out of every attendance calculation, including averages and shortage counts, until the register is verified. An empty attendance must never be treated as zero.

## AT-4 Average attendance of a group

The average attendance of a department, subject or any other group is the arithmetic mean of the recorded attendance values in the group, leaving out records whose attendance is not recorded (AT-3). It is rounded to two decimal places in reports.

## AT-5 Medical and duty leave

A student who is absent on medical grounds must submit a certificate from a registered medical practitioner within five working days of returning to class. Duty leave is granted for representing the institute in approved technical, cultural or sports events, up to a maximum of ten working days in a semester. Classes missed on approved duty leave are already credited before the attendance percentage is recorded, so no further adjustment is made in reports.

## AT-6 Monitoring and notification

Class teachers publish attendance at the end of every month. A student whose attendance in any subject falls below the requirement at a monthly review receives a written notice, and the parent or guardian is informed after the second consecutive notice. The figures used for end-of-semester decisions are the final values recorded in the student performance data.
